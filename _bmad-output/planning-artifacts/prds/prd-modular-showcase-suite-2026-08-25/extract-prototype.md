## 1. Modules / Screens

All app screens live under `/app/*` (file-based routes in `src/routes/`). `/` redirects to `/login` or `/app` based on the stored auth user; `/app` is guarded (`beforeLoad` redirect to `/login` when `oneedu.auth.user` is absent). Every page renders inside `AppShell` (sidebar + header) and behind `ModuleGate` (per-tenant module entitlement gate). Many pages render **role-variant views** from the same route.

### 1.2 Dashboards (`/app`, `app.index.tsx`) — one route, five variants

- **Student dashboard**: GPA / attendance / pending tasks / outstanding fees stat cards; upcoming assignments; notifications; grade trend bar chart; swim-award progress card (with links to printable certificates) when the student has award progress. Minor students see a **guardian-linked banner** (guardian name, approvals model); adult (18+) self-managed students see a **self-managed banner** with quick actions (Courses, Pay fees, Enrol).
- **Parent dashboard**: children-linked / institutes-unified / avg attendance / total dues cards; per-child cards (attendance, GPA, dues, next class, chips for every enrolled institute); swim awards & certificates card across children.
- **Teacher dashboard**: classes / students / to-grade / AI-flagged-at-risk cards; "My Appraisal" card (blended score + recent family reviews, links to full appraisal); today's schedule with "Start class"; AI suggestions panel. **Swim-coach variant**: sessions-this-week / swimmers / today's sessions / pools cards + "Today's pool" (or next training day) session list linking into pool sessions.
- **Institute admin dashboard** (adminScope="institute"): scoping banner; institute About card (vision/mission/description from branding, with "Edit branding" link); roster size / outstanding fees / collected / at-risk cards; **class capacity planning** (per-course seat fill %, under-filled/low/full flags, revenue framing); recent record-book activity (needs-ack counter); at-risk student list. All data filtered to the admin's own tenant.
- **Global admin dashboard**: cross-tenant active tenants / total students / MRR / open leads cards; revenue trend and attendance trend charts; at-risk students (AI prediction) list across tenants.
- **Swim-club admin dashboard** (`isSwimAdmin`): weekly sessions / swimmers / coaches / open incidents cards; quick cards (Coaches & Sessions, Summary Reports, Record Books); **capacity planning per pool session** (seat fill, coach staffing, understaffed flags, consolidation suggestion linking to the target session); today's/next pool session list; open incidents panel; recent coach cover panel.

## 2. Roles & Permissions

### 2.1 Role model

- Base `Role` union: `student | parent | teacher | admin` (`mockData.ts`).
- Admin split via `adminScope`: `"global"` (platform super-admin, all tenants) vs `"institute"` (bound to one `institutionId`).
- **Swim overlay**: `meta.discipline === "Swimming"` flips an account into the single-purpose swim-club experience (`isSwimUser`); `meta.discipline === "Swimming"` + role `admin` = swim-club admin (`isSwimAdmin`). Swim coach = role `teacher` + discipline flag; scoped by name to her own sessions (`isSwimCoach`, `sessionsForCoach`).
- **Guardianship model** for students: `dob` + `selfManaged`. Under-18 = guardian-linked (guardian approves enrolments/payments/course selection; cannot rate teachers; cannot self-acknowledge SRB entries). 18+ = self-managed (own fees, own SRB acknowledgements, may rate teachers).
- **Co-parent model**: two guardians per child (`swimmerGuardians`), separate logins, both see the same children, each pays independently (`paidBy` on invoices).
- Menus are role-derived (`menusByRole` in `menus.ts`) then filtered by module entitlements and demo-hidden-nav; swim accounts get curated club menus (`swimCoachMenu`, `swimAdminMenu`) whose items are all moduleId "core" (never gated).

### 2.3 What each role can access (as enforced in code)

- **Student (minor)**: dashboard, courses, attendance (own), calendar, LMS, assignments (submit), grades, own record book (read + reply; cannot acknowledge), appraisals **hidden** from menu and blocked in-page, course selection, fees (pay), messages, marketplace, profile.

Co-parent identical with own login.
- **Teacher**: dashboard, My Classes, take attendance (bulk + single), calendar, LMS, grading, students, record books (post entries, require-ack, pin, delete, reply), teacher training (enrol, complete, certificate), own appraisal (redirected to own page), messages, AI insights, profile.

- **Global admin**: all tenants and collections; tenant onboarding + wizard; module entitlements for any tenant; platform landing branding; Demo-presentation controls (hide demo accounts, hide nav items); compliance, migration, reports, marketing, users, marketplace, finance across tenants.
- **Swim coach**: curated club menu (Dashboard, Swim Club, Courses & Awards, Record Books, Competitive Squad, Summary Reports, Coach Education, My Appraisal, Messages, Profile). Sees only sessions where she is on the *effective* roster (cover swaps grant/remove access), only her swimmers everywhere (students, SRB, squad, reports).
- **Swim-club admin**: curated club menu (adds Coaches & Sessions, Swimmers, Fees & Finance, Fees & Pricing, Billing & Invoicing, Marketing & CRM, Users & Roles, Migration, Compliance, Branding, Settings, LMS, Coach Training, Coach Appraisals). Sees the whole club but only the club (invoices filtered to C-SWIM, leads to program "Swim", users to club staff, appraisals to coaches).
- **Route-level gates observed**: `/app/attendance/take` (teacher/admin only, redirect), `/app/coaching` (swim admin only), `/app/pricing` & `/app/billing` (admins only), `/app/branding` (admins only), `/app/awards` & `/app/swim-reports` (swim accounts only), ModuleGate on every route by tenant entitlements.

## 3. Data Model (localStorage store)

### 3.1 Store mechanics (`src/lib/store.ts`)

- Single reactive store on `useSyncExternalStore`; persisted whole-state JSON at **`STORAGE_KEY = "oneedu.store.v3"`**.
- **Seed versioning**: `SEED_VERSION_KEY = "oneedu.store.seedver"`, current `SEED_VERSION = "2026-07-billing-perchild"` — bumping the seed version auto-reseeds stale browsers on deploy *without* renaming the storage key; loads merge per-key over a fresh seed so new collections never break old saves.
- API: `useCollection(key)`, `addItem`, `updateItem(predicate, patch)`, `removeItem(predicate)`, `resetStore()` (the "Reset demo" button), `nextId(prefix, key)` (monotonic id generator, prefix-aware).
- Other localStorage keys: `oneedu.auth.user` (session), `oneedu.prefs.v1` (theme/locale/currency), `oneedu.tenant.modules.v1` (entitlements per tenant), `oneedu.tour` (guided-demo state), `oneedu.sidebar.collapsedGroups`.

- **invoices** (`InvoiceRow`: id, date, desc, amount, status Paid/Due/Upcoming, method, optional studentId, institutionId/Name, **courseId** (programme tag e.g. C-SWIM), **paidBy** (co-parent), classLabel, **periodMonth** (billing-run linkage)).

### 3.3 Relationship highlights

- Student `S-XXXX` is the platform-wide primary key; `studentEnrollments` + runtime `enrollments` give a student N institute relationships, each preserving a **legacy ID/system** from the institute's prior LMS.
- Invoices/SRB entries are tagged with `institutionId` (tenant isolation) and optionally `courseId` (programme isolation, e.g. swim).
- Pool sessions reference pool + zone + lanes + coach names + swimmer ids; `swimmerMoves`/`coachMoves`/`sessionRosters` overlay the static timetable to produce effective rosters everywhere (attendance, capacity, squad, access control).
- Award certification writes `awardProgress.certifiedAt` and emits chat messages to `guardiansForSwimmer(studentId)`.
- The billing engine derives invoices at read time from selection + plans + discounts + closures + credits, and "Issue" materialises them into `invoices` with `periodMonth` linkage back to the itemised breakdown.
