Run note: every direct WebFetch to *.gov.au (asqa.gov.au, ncver.edu.au, usi.gov.au) timed out this run, and legacy.asqa.gov.au did not resolve. Australian evidence therefore comes from search-engine extracts that quote official pages, not from full-page reads. A claim is tagged **verified** only when official text was quoted in the retrieved extract and a second document agrees, or when the extract is unambiguous official wording. Single official extracts are tagged **likely**. Fetches of the SSG terms PDF returned binary and could not be parsed (no Python on host), and ask.gov.sg returned HTTP 403.

## Contradictions / surprises
- **Assessment retention** went from 6 months (ASQA general direction under Standards 2015) to **2 years** (Standards 2025). Many legacy RTO policies still say 6 months.
- **VET attendance under the National Code is not universal.** It applies only when ASQA imposes it (21 providers as at Jan 2019). ASQA's 2026 ADC focus on "attendance and progress" suggests tightening. Do not hard-code attendance monitoring as mandatory for all AU CRICOS VET tenants. Make it a configurable condition.
- **SG 75% funding attendance**: SSG removed the trainee-level 75% rule from 1 Jan 2024 in favour of course-level thresholds, yet many provider T&Cs and a 2026 "SSI FAQs" still state 75%. Treat the threshold as **per-course configurable**.
- **AVETMISS 9.0** was not found. Release 8.0 (effective 1 Jan 2018) appears to be current. The brief's "9.0?" hypothesis is unsupported.
- **TVA cadence**: the brief assumed annual fee-for-service reporting by end of February. Evidence shows a **quarterly option** for fee-for-service since 2018 and quarterly mandatory government-funded reporting via STAs. The annual mandatory date was not confirmed.
- **Malaysia's national skills data system is MySPIKE**, not "MyTVET".
- **TPGateway deadlines**: IBF gives 120 days for providers and 105 days for FI in-house courses. The SSG-wide figure was not confirmed.
