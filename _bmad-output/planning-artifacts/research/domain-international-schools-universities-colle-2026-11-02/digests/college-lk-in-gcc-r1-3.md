### India
- [IN][regulatory] NCVET recognises, regulates and monitors Awarding Bodies (ABs) and Assessment Agencies (AAs) for long- and short-term skilling. It also recognises "Skill-related Information Providers". "Dual" recognition (AB plus AA) exists. Recent Dual ABs include Flipkart (Dec 2025) and KKHSOU (Mar 2026). | source: https://ncvet.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/NCVT-News_Oct-25-March-26-Final-2.pdf ; https://ncvet.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/flipkart-awarding-body-19-december-2025.pdf (snippets) | NCVET | 2026 | accessed 2026-11-02 | confidence: likely | class: regulatory
- [IN][grading] The revised NSQF was notified on 6 June 2023, after UGC notified the National Credit Framework (NCrF) on 10 April 2023. It has eight levels (1–8) plus half-levels, including Level 4.5. 1 credit = 30 notional hours, and a full year = 40 credits = 1200 hours. ABs are advised to size qualifications in multiples of 30 hours. Minimum-hour ranges by type: micro-credentials 7.5–30 h; National Occupational Standards >30–210 h. | source: https://ncvet.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/MoM-of-32nd-NSQC-meeting.pdf ; https://ncvet.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/MoM-37th-NSQC-Meeting.pdf ; https://ncvet.gov.in/?p=11295 (snippets) | NCVET | 2023–2024 | accessed 2026-11-02 | confidence: verified for 30 h/credit (NCVET minutes plus an independent academic source agree); likely for 8 levels/4.5 | class: grading

### UAE
- [AE][regulatory] KHDA (Dubai) issues an "Educational Services Permit for a Training Institute". It is valid for 1 year and must be renewed annually, at least 30 days before expiry. Initial Approval becomes void if the permit is not obtained within the initial-approval period. Applications go via the KHDA portal (mainland applicants can also use Invest in Dubai). A trade licence (DET or free zone) is a prerequisite. Courses are approved separately from the permit. | source: https://web.khda.gov.ae/en/Services/Training-Institute-Permit-Services/Issuing-an-Educational-Services-Permit-for-a-Train (snippet) | KHDA | n.d. | accessed 2026-11-02 | confidence: likely | class: regulatory

### Saudi Arabia

Executive rules are on Ethaq Plus (ethaqplus.tvtc.gov.sa). Only the owner, MD, board chair or GM may apply, authenticating with national ID number plus DOB and an OTP to the Absher-registered phone. ## Contradictions / surprises
- Saudi licence validity is 5 years (TVTC) against 1 year with annual renewal for Dubai (KHDA). Tenant licence-expiry tracking needs per-jurisdiction periods.
- India's NSQF is no longer integer-only (Level 4.5 exists), so a level field must be decimal. Credits are fixed at 30 notional hours, which is useful for auto-computing credits from course hours.
- India's biometric attendance covers trainers and assessors as well as candidates (daily AEBAS). Fraud in Aadhaar biometric attendance was reported in 2026 news (nextias, 10-02-2026).
- The QFEmirates level count for TVET conflicts between vendor guides (1–5 vs 1–8). The official framework is believed to be 10 levels (unverified this run).
- Private Dual AB status in India: one vendor (Raysolute) says it is restricted to government bodies, but Flipkart was recognised as a Dual AB in Dec 2025.
- Saudi TVTC treats "manipulation of records/trainee results" as a top violation category. An immutable audit log on attendance and grades is a selling point.
