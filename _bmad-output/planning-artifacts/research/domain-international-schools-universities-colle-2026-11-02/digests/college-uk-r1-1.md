### ULN / LRS / PLR (Q2)
- [UK-ENG][identifier] The ULN is a **10-digit** reference linked to the learner's **Personal Learning Record (PLR)**. The PLR is an online record of qualifications and achievements. Providers collect ULNs on behalf of learners **aged 14 and above**. LRS is now run by DfE (ESFA-closure notice on the page). Access is via the registered **LRS Organisation Portal**. Batch files are CSV or XML, and web services can link an MIS to the learner register to search by ULN and register new ULNs. Providers must show every learner the privacy notice, and learners cannot opt out of ULN/PLR sharing. | source: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/learner-registration-bodies-user-guide/using-the-learning-records-service-lrs-for-learning-providers-and-local-authorities | DfE | updated 9-May-2024 | accessed 2026-11-02 | confidence: verified | class: identifier
- [UK-ENG][identifier] ILR field `ULN`: "Collected for all learners". Valid range is 1000000000–9999999999, and **9999999999** is the "unknown" placeholder. The placeholder is allowed freely until 1 January of the current year. After that it is allowed only for Community Learning or non-funded aims, aims that started fewer than 60 days before file preparation, or aims shorter than 5 days. It is never allowed for FundModel 36 with LearnDelFAMType ACT1 (levy/contract-for-services apprentices) or for Advanced Learner Loan learners. The LRS support contact is LRS.Support@education.gov.uk. | source: https://guidance.submit-learner-data.service.gov.uk/25-26/ilr/entity/Learner/field/ULN | DfE | 2025 | accessed 2026-11-02 | confidence: verified | class: identifier

### Attendance: 16-19 and apprenticeships (Q3)

### UKVI Student / Child Student (Q4)
- [UK][attendance] **The 85% review and 70% withdrawal thresholds apply only to HIGHER EDUCATION PROVIDERS (HEPs), not to FE colleges or schools.** Band 1 means "Study at a higher education provider at RQF level 5 ... and below". Students in this band must attend at least 15 hours a week of daytime classroom study (08:00–18:00, Mon–Fri). If attendance is below 85% in any month, the sponsor reviews it and annotates the record. If attendance is below 70% for 3 consecutive months, the sponsor must withdraw sponsorship unless there are exceptional, evidenced reasons. Para 7.15: "Sponsors which are not higher education providers must continue to ... [apply] the current attendance monitoring policy, set out at rows d. and e." | source: https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/690b3b0eb04a520c505184c2/Sponsorship+Duties+.pdf (Student Sponsor Guidance Document 2: Sponsorship Duties, "Version 10/2026 – to be used from 8 October 2026", paras 7.13–7.23) | Home Office / UKVI | Oct-2026 | accessed 2026-11-02 | confidence: verified | class: attendance
- [UK][attendance] **Non-HEP sponsors (FE colleges, independent schools)** use the contact-point model:
  - Sponsors with Probationary Sponsor status must tell UKVI within **10 working days** if a student misses **10 consecutive expected contact points** (row d).
  - Sponsors with full Student Sponsor status can either report the tenth missed contact, or set **two checkpoints in any rolling 12-month period** and report students who missed 10 consecutive expected contacts without permission (row e).
  - Authorised absences (illness, pregnancy and so on) need not be reported.
  - If the sponsor decides not to withdraw after 10 missed contacts, it must keep evidence and reasoning; UKVI says this "should be very rare" (para 7.10).

  | source: same PDF | Oct-2026 | confidence: verified | class: attendance
- [UK][attendance] Below-degree courses must be full-time, with at least 15 hours a week of classroom-based daytime study (08:00–18:00, Mon–Fri) excluding scheduled breaks (para 4.6c). This applies to all sponsors, not only HEPs. | source: same PDF | confidence: verified | class: attendance
- [UK][attendance] Sponsors "must report on all Students and Child Students" (para 7.1). Record keeping includes a "Record of the migrant's absence/attendance" (para 2.19). ## Contradictions / surprises
- **The 85%/70% UKVI thresholds are not an FE rule.** They apply only to HEPs (Band 1 = HEP at RQF 5 and below). FE colleges and independent schools must still use the older "10 consecutive expected contact points" rule, reporting within 10 working days. The brief's assumption needs correcting in the product: the College/FE pack should use the contact-point model, and the HE pack the engagement-band model.
- **Apprenticeships and adult skills now sit with DWP, not DfE** (from Sept 2025), and Skills England moved with them. 16-19 and HE stay with DfE. FE regulatory metadata should therefore carry two owning departments.
- **The 20% OTJT rule is gone for starts from 1 Aug 2025.** It is replaced by a per-standard published minimum hours figure (floor 187 hours after prior learning), and minimum duration fell from 12 to 8 months. Carry-in learners stay on the 20% / 6 hours-a-week rule, so a product must support both rules side by side.
- **Colleges have no statutory attendance reporting to DfE** (Children's Commissioner, Sept 2026). This is a policy gap DfE says it will close, so it may become a near-future requirement worth designing for.
- R14 date discrepancy for 2025-26: DfE says the final ILR and claim are due 23-Oct-2026, while the Bud vendor guide says R14 is 22-Oct-2026 (unresolved; the DfE date is preferred).
- KCSIE 2026 (Sept 2026) explicitly includes Independent Training Providers, but para 312 says some "musts" for colleges do not apply to them. SCR fields may need provider-type variants.
