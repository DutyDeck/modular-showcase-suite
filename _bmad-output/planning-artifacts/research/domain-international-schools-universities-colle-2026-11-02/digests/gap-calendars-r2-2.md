## Contradictions / surprises
- All three Sri Lankan international schools whose dates were read (CIS, BSC, EMS) run late Aug to Jun/Jul with three terms (Michaelmas/Lent/Summer or First/Second/Third). None runs Jan–Dec. The "local-run international schools run Jan–Dec" hypothesis was not confirmed for any named school. Lyceum shows a September intake.
- Sri Lankan international schools start in late August (24–31 Aug), not September. Seed data should use late August, not "September".
- The Sri Lankan government school year is nominally Jan–Dec but split into term "phases" (e.g. 3rd term phase 2 Sep–Dec 2026). The year labels "2025-26" for exams suggest the calendar is still catching up after earlier disruption.
- In the UAE, schools and universities share the same 31 Aug 2026 start, and both calendars were set for three years (2026-29). Outlets disagree on the university winter break.
- Malaysia's January return happened with the 2026 session (11/12 Jan 2026, split by state group), not 2025.
- Saudi public schools start earlier than other GCC systems (23 Aug 2026), while KSU starts 30 Aug. Hijri dates in secondary sources are inconsistent (Safar vs Rabi' I).
- Qatar's winter and autumn break dates conflict between Doha News and mdares.ai.
- Cambridge is withdrawing some syllabuses from the India-only March series from 2027.
