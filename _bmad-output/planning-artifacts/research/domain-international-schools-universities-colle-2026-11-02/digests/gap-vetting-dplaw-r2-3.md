# Digest: gap-fill vetting, Noor, DP law, MIS incumbents

Run date: 2026-11-02. About 27 tool calls. Items 1, 3 and 6 were prioritised. Items 4, 5 and 7 (international) are thin.

## Findings

### 2. Abu Dhabi / ADEK
- [UAE-AbuDhabi][good conduct] ADEK makes a criminal record check or certificate of good conduct "a fundamental requirement" for candidates applying to work in private schools. UAE residents need a certificate from UAE authorities. Applicants from abroad need one "from the relevant authorities in their home country". Schools upload certificates through the School Staff Licensing Portal, and the documents are verified with the issuing authorities. No lookback period is stated. | source: https://gulfnews.com/uae/education/abu-dhabi-mandates-criminal-record-checks-for-private-school-teachers-1.500238169 | Gulf News | updated 2025-08-19 | accessed 2026-11-02 | confidence: likely (press report of an ADEK requirement; I did not read the ADEK policy text) | class: vetting
- [UAE-AbuDhabi][licensing portal] ADEK's staff portal is PASS (Private Schools Abu Dhabi Staff System, pass.adek.gov.ae). It handles staff profile and document verification, ADEK approval, renewals, amendments and promotions. The staff record follows the person when they change school. It also hosts a Teacher Talent Pool. | source: https://pass.adek.gov.ae/ ; https://www.adek.gov.ae/Media-Centre/News/ADEK-aims-to-provide-an-advanced-and-friendly-user-system-for-private-school-employees | ADEK | n.d. | accessed 2026-11-02 (snippets) | confidence: likely | class: vetting

## Contradictions / surprises
- The Dubai good-conduct scope is inconsistent across sources. The National (2018) says every country worked in during the past 5 years. Gulf News quotes KHDA saying "the country they last worked in, basically". KHDA's own e-service snippet gives no country or lookback rule. ADEK (2025) asks for the home country only, or the UAE for residents.
- KHDA's 2025 "Appointment Notice" replaced the Appointment Letter. Approval is per school, with a 90-day cooling-off for mid-term leavers and an Exit Survey dependency. A PRD should model this as per-school, per-transfer staff approval, not a one-off licence.
- The UK DUAA data-protection core is already in force (5 Feb 2026), not still pending. ICO functions moved to the Information Commission on 30 Sep 2026, so PRD text should refer to the "Information Commission".
- The UAE PDPL is still not enforceable more than 5 years after enactment, because the Executive Regulations have not been issued. Vendor sites claiming specific Cabinet Decisions are unreliable.
- Malaysia's DPO and breach-notification date is 1 June 2025, as the brief assumed. One source (Rödl) says 1 July 2025, but that is an outlier.
- Noor: I found no official text making Noor mandatory for international schools' attendance or grades. The evidence only shows Noor used for registration (e.g. 2023 Grade 1 in private and international schools).
