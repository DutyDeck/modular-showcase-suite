### SAUDI ARABIA

It was retained for 2023-24. On **2025-08-06**, the Cabinet approved a return to **two semesters for public schools from 2025-26**. "Private and international schools, universities, and vocational institutions will retain the flexibility to choose the academic model that best suits their needs." The term model therefore varies by university and must be configurable per tenant. | source: https://educationmiddleeast.com/?p=11525 ; https://tu.edu.sa/En/University/News/23934/TU-Council-approves-the-transformation-plan-for-the-three-semester-system | Education Middle East; Taif University | 2025-08-06 | accessed 2026-11-02 | confidence: verified (trade press read plus a university source on adoption) | class: calendar

## Contradictions / surprises
- **Sri Lanka PDPA is still not fully in force.** The core obligations (Part I, Part III) now start on **2027-01-01** (Gazette 2498/16) after the March 2025 date was revoked. The DPA's own website still shows March 2025. Part II rights and Part VII penalties have no known commencement order.
- **India DPDP:** consent-manager registration starts on **2026-11-13** (imminent), but the substantive obligations, including verifiable parental consent for under-18s, only start on **2027-05-13**. Most HE students are 18 or older, but first-year students can be 17.
- **Saudi Arabia reversed the 3-semester system for public schools in 2025-26**, while universities keep discretion. The calendar must be configurable per university: 2 or 3 terms.
- **UAE "UQAIC" in the brief should be "UQAIB".** Sources disagree on whether free-zone HEIs need CAA accreditation. A 2010 KHDA report says it is voluntary, Khaleej Times says it is either UQAIB or CAA, and a Meydan FZ guide claims CAA is mandatory.
- **APAAR:** officially "not mandatory", yet UGC compels HEIs to map credits to APAAR IDs on NAD-ABC. In practice it is an institutional obligation, not a student one.
- **Qatar Law 13/2016:** sources give different effective dates (29 Dec 2016, gazetted 3 Feb 2017, or "effective 2018") and different regulator names.
- **UAE institution counts differ:** 54 integrated, 65 on the unified registration system, 66 reporting enrolment.
