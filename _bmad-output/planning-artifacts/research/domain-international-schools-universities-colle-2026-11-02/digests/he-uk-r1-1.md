- [UK][attendance] Para 7.15 says HE providers use an **"academic engagement policy"**, which "replaces the current attendance monitoring policy for such sponsors" (in effect since 5 Oct 2020, para 7.16). Non-HE sponsors still use the 10-consecutive-missed-contact-points model. | source: Doc 2 v10/2026 (above) | UKVI | 2026-10-08 | accessed 2026-11-02 | confidence: verified | class: attendance
- [UK][attendance] **Band 1** covers HE study at RQF level 5 (SCQF 8) and below. The rule is a "minimum of 15 hours of daytime (08:00 to 18:00, Monday to Friday) classroom-based study per week". If a student is below **85% attendance in any month**, the sponsor reviews the absence and annotates the record. If attendance is "below **70% for three consecutive months**, the sponsor must withdraw sponsorship" unless there are exceptional, evidenced reasons (paras 7.22–7.23). | source: Doc 2 v10/2026 | UKVI | 2026-10-08 | accessed 2026-11-02 | confidence: verified | class: attendance
- [UK][attendance] **Band 2** covers RQF 6 (SCQF 9/10), RQF 7 taught and research (SCQF 11) and RQF 8 (SCQF 12). The sponsor needs "a single academic engagement policy" applied consistently to international students. Engagement includes attending required lectures, seminars or tutorials (face-to-face or remote), lab work, research or fieldwork, and submitting assignments or attending exams (7.24–7.25). The sponsor must show that "the maximum period of non-engagement before intervention takes place does not exceed **60 calendar days** in duration (**30 calendar days** for students studying by remote delivery), excepting recognised vacation periods, and excluding post-graduate research and doctoral students" (7.27). **Band 3**: no monitoring during vacation or post-study periods (7.30). | source: Doc 2 v10/2026 | UKVI | 2026-10-08 | accessed 2026-11-02 | confidence: verified | class: attendance
- [UK][attendance] Para 7.20: "Where the student fails to re-engage with their studies within 60 calendar days (30 days for remote delivery)... of the first contact from their sponsor regarding their lack of academic engagement, the sponsor must withdraw sponsorship except in exceptional circumstances." Para 7.19: the sponsor must annotate the student's record with the reason and the re-engagement steps. Product implication: the system needs an engagement-event log, a "first contact" timestamp, and 60/30-day clocks that pause for vacations. | source: Doc 2 v10/2026 | UKVI | 2026-10-08 | accessed 2026-11-02 | confidence: verified | class: attendance
- [UK][attendance] Examples of "expected contact points" (table rows d/e): lessons, lectures, tutorials, seminars; tests, exams, assessment boards; supervisor or personal tutor meetings; research panel meetings, writing-up seminars, doctoral workshops; viva; welfare or international student adviser appointments; submitting assessed or unassessed coursework, interim dissertations or reports; and "registration (for enrolment or matriculation)". | source: Doc 2 v10/2026 | UKVI | 2026-10-08 | accessed 2026-11-02 | confidence: verified | class: attendance
- [UK][attendance] Reporting deadlines in the "Changes to student circumstances table" (via the Sponsorship Management System, SMS). **10 working days** applies to each of these: (a) withdrawal before travel; (c) non-enrolment, counted "within 10 working days of the enrolment period ending, including the reasons"; (d) Probationary Sponsors report "within 10 working days of the last expected contact point, or the date the student has stopped academically engaging"; (f) deferral over 60 calendar days, counted from agreeing the deferral, with sponsorship withdrawn; (g) withdrawing a student, counted from knowing; (h) stopping sponsorship. Under row (e), a sponsor with full Student Sponsor status can either "Report a tenth missed contact, or the date the student has stopped academically engaging whenever it occurs during the year; or Set **two checkpoints during any rolling 12-month period**". | source: Doc 2 v10/2026 | UKVI | 2026-10-08 | accessed 2026-11-02 | confidence: verified | class: attendance
- [UK][attendance] Other deadlines: organisational changes (for example, key personnel) must be reported "within **20 working days**". A UKVI request for a spreadsheet of students and their addresses must be answered "within **21 days**" (para 2.5). | source: Doc 2 v10/2026 | UKVI | 2026-10-08 | accessed 2026-11-02 | confidence: verified | class: regulatory
- [UK][attendance] Record keeping (Doc 2 section "Specific record-keeping duties", paras 2.4–2.15). The student guidance has no "Appendix D"; Appendix D is the Worker-route term. Sponsors keep paper or electronic records and produce them on request (2.6). Retention runs for the period of sponsorship until the earlier of "one year has passed from the date on which your sponsorship of the student ended" or compliance-officer approval (2.7). Sponsors must keep copies of passport identity and status pages (2.10) and record the date of UK entry (2.13). eVisas have been issued instead of BRPs for decisions after 31 Oct 2024 (2.14). | source: Doc 2 v10/2026 | UKVI | 2026-10-08 | accessed 2026-11-02 | confidence: verified | class: attendance
- [UK][attendance] The CAS "is valid for six months from the date" of assignment (around line 701 of Doc 2). A withdrawn or cancelled CAS fee is not refunded (5.134). | source: Doc 2 v10/2026 | UKVI | 2026-10-08 | accessed 2026-11-02 | confidence: verified | class: attendance
- [UK][attendance] **Basic Compliance Assessment** (Doc 3 "Student sponsor compliance", Version 06/2026, used from 01/06/2026, para 2.14). For applications on or after 1 Jun 2026: visa refusal rate **<5%**, enrolment rate **≥95%**, course completion rate **≥85%**. Completion rises to **≥90%** from 1 Jun 2027. Before 1 Jun 2026 the thresholds were <10% / ≥90% / ≥85%. ## Contradictions / surprises
- UKVI "attendance monitoring" no longer applies to HE providers. They run a self-defined **academic engagement policy** (Band 2), with a 60-day (30 for remote) maximum before intervention. The 10-missed-contacts rule now applies only to non-HE sponsors, plus Band 1 (sub-degree) percentage attendance of 85% and 70%. A UK HE pack should model engagement events, not just class attendance.
- The BCA tightened sharply on 1 Jun 2026: refusal <5% (was <10%), enrolment ≥95% (was ≥90%), and completion ≥90% from Jun 2027. A RAG band sits just above each threshold. This is a strong case for a "UKVI compliance dashboard" feature.
- There is a new UKVI **Document 4 "Higher Education regulatory reform"** that I did not read. It may change sponsor/OfS linkage.
- In-year HESA collection has slipped. The OfS had earlier aimed for 2027-28, but the first full in-year collection is now "Winter 2028" against the 2028-29 data model. Until then the Student return is annual.
- The brief's "Appendix D" (record keeping) is a Worker-route term. Student-route record keeping sits in Doc 2 section 2, "Specific record-keeping duties", with retention of 1 year after sponsorship ends.
- The identifier changed: Data Futures uses a 17-character **SID** that embeds the UKPRN, replacing the 13-digit HUSID. Legacy HUSIDs persist for continuing students.
