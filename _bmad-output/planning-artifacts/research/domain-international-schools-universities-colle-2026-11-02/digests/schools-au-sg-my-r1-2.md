Run: r1-2, accessed 2026-11-02. Budget: ~22 tool calls. Starred items were done first. legislation.gov.au gave back only its table of contents, education.gov.au timed out, and aifs.gov.au and raisingchildren.net.au returned 403.

## Findings

### Australia: overseas students in schools (ESOS / National Code 2018)
- [AU][attendance] Standard 8.1 of the National Code 2018 requires a CRICOS school to monitor each overseas student's course progress and, where it applies, attendance for each course. A NSW school applies this for its school-sector international students. | source: https://www.meriden.nsw.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Monitoring-Course-Attendance-Policy_November-2023.pdf | Meriden (NSW CRICOS school) | 10/11/2023 | accessed 2026-11-02 | confidence: likely (provider policy quoting the Standard; the Code text itself did not load) | class: attendance
- [AU][attendance] The minimum satisfactory attendance is **80 per cent of scheduled contact hours per study period, or higher if a state or territory specifies it**. Two independent school policies (NSW and WA) say this, and the Commonwealth Ombudsman gives the same 80% for ELICOS. | source: Meriden PDF above; https://perthcollege.wa.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Monitoring-Course-Attendance-Policy.pdf ; https://www.ombudsman.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0031/289390/Overseas-Students-provider-e-news-edition-15-April-2019.pdf | Meriden; Perth College; Commonwealth Ombudsman | 2023; 2026-05; 2019-04-15 | accessed 2026-11-02 | confidence: verified (two independent sources agree) | class: attendance
- [AU][attendance] Standard 8.15 lets a school decide **not to report** a student who is still attending at least **70%** of scheduled contact hours. Product rule: two thresholds (80% at-risk, 70% must-report), each set per study period. | source: Meriden PDF | 2023 | confidence: likely | class: attendance
- [AU][attendance] Standard 8.13 requires the school to send a written **Notice of Intention to Report** that gives its reasons. The student then has **20 working days** to use the school's complaints and appeals process (Standard 10). Standard 8.14 says the school may report unsatisfactory attendance in **PRISMS** only after that 20-working-day period passes without an appeal, or after an appeal fails or the student withdraws from it. | source: Meriden PDF | 2023 | confidence: likely | class: attendance
- [AU][attendance] Standard 8.17 says that if a school extends a student's enrolment, it must tell the student to contact the **Department of Home Affairs** about visa impacts. | source: Meriden PDF | 2023 | confidence: likely | class: attendance
- [AU][calendar] Meriden defines its international-student "study periods" as **Semester 1 and Semester 2**, so a school-sector study period can be a semester rather than a term. The 80% test runs per study period. | source: Meriden PDF | 2023 | confidence: likely | class: attendance
- [AU][regulatory] Standard 5 (younger overseas students) defines a **CAAW** as a "Confirmation of Appropriate Accommodation and Welfare". It is issued for students under 18 when the school takes on welfare responsibility under the Migration Regulations. Standard 5.1 requires compliance with Commonwealth and state child-protection laws. Standard 5.3 requires the school to approve accommodation, support and general welfare arrangements. Standard 5.4 requires reasonable steps to make new arrangements if the school can no longer approve them. | source: https://www.meriden.nsw.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Younger-International-Students-Policy-March-2024.pdf ; https://perthcollege.wa.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Younger-International-Students-Policy.pdf | school policies (from search extracts, not read in full) | 2024-03; 2026-05 | confidence: likely | class: regulatory
- [AU][regulatory] No CAAW is needed when a parent or a suitable nominated relative takes responsibility for welfare. School responsibility under a CAAW ends when the student turns 18, stops the enrolment and leaves Australia, or moves under another provider's CAAW. | source: https://internationaleducation.gov.au/Regulatory-Information/Education-Services-for-Overseas-Students-ESOS-Legislative-Framework/National-Code/nationalcodepartd/Pages/ExplanatoryguideD5.aspx (older National Code explanatory guide) plus school policies | Dept of Education (legacy) | n.d. | confidence: likely (the official guide is for an earlier Code edition) | class: regulatory
- [AU][regulatory] VRQA (Victoria) applies Child Safe Standards to international students' living arrangements in Victorian schools. This adds a state layer on top of Standard 5. | source: https://vrqa.vic.gov.au/childsafe/Pages/schools.aspx | VRQA | n.d. | confidence: likely (search snippet only) | class: regulatory

### Australia: statutory data
- [AU][data-return] The **Non-government Schools Census** is an annual collection. Non-government schools that receive Australian Government recurrent funding must complete it, and it is legally required under **s77 Australian Education Act 2013**. 2026 dates: opens **Mon 3 Aug 2026** in the **Education Funding System (EFS)**; **census day Fri 7 Aug 2026**; data and declaration due **Fri 14 Aug 2026**. The student reference period is the **20 school days ending on census day**. Staff are counted if employed or engaged on census day. Earlier census days were Fri 2 Aug 2024 and Fri 1 Aug 2025; ABS describes the census date as the first Friday in August. | source: https://content.educationhub.gov.au/system/files/2026-07/2026%20Non-government%20schools%20census%20guide%20v.1.pdf ; https://schools.education.gov.au/schoolshub/help/files/2024.non-government.schools.census.requirements | Dept of Education | 2026-07 | confidence: likely (official guide, but read via search extract and not opened) | class: data-return
- [AU][attendance] ACARA's **National Standards for Student Attendance Data Reporting**, **Third Edition (updated July 2020)**, apply to **Years 1–10** in government, Catholic and independent schools. The listed components are: attendance-rate formula (actual days in attendance ÷ possible school days), part-day absences, treatment of incidents/absences, and data collection period. | source: https://acara.edu.au/reporting/national-standards-for-student-attendance-data-reporting | ACARA | n.d. | confidence: verified (page read) | class: attendance
- [AU][attendance] The 2020 revision changed the counting rules so that a **partial absence of more than 120 minutes counts as a half-day absence**. An "attendance level" KPM (share of students attending at least 90%) was added in 2015. | source: search extract referring to ACARA/My School caveats (https://myschool.edu.au/media/1966/2024-agde-non-government-student-attendance-caveats.pdf) | ACARA | 2024 | confidence: likely (the 90% figure is unverified) | class: attendance
- [AU][identifier] Unverified belief: Australia has no national school student ID. States use their own, for example the NSW Student Number, the Victorian Student Number (VSN) and the QLD LUI (Learner Unique Identifier, via QCAA). School students doing VET (VETiS) need a **USI**. | source: training-data hypothesis | confidence: unverified | class: identifier

### Singapore
- [SG][regulatory] Foreigners studying full-time at a **Foreign System School (FSS) or Privately-Funded School** need an ICA **Student's Pass**. Holders of a Dependant's Pass, a Long-Term Visit Pass or an Immigration Exemption Order are exempt. **The school must be EduTrust-certified, or ICA will not consider the application.** | source: https://www.ica.gov.sg/reside/STP/apply/fss | ICA | last updated 29 Oct 2026 | accessed 2026-11-02 | confidence: verified | class: regulatory
- [SG][regulatory] Application inputs are the **Registration Acknowledgement Letter** from the school, travel document biodata page, birth certificate, parents' details, Singapore address, a photo taken within 3 months, and vaccination records (submitted to the Communicable Diseases Agency) for foreign-born applicants aged 12 or under. New applications go in **2–3 months** before the course starts. Renewals go in **4 weeks** before expiry; transfers at least 4 weeks before the new course. The fee is **S$45**. Processing takes about 1 week (2 weeks if a visa is required). Approval produces an In-Principle Approval (IPA) letter. Online submission runs through ICA's portal; the link path contains "solar-plus". | source: same | ICA | 29 Oct 2026 | confidence: verified | class: regulatory
- [SG][regulatory] Singapore citizens need MOE approval to enrol in an FSS. | source: search extracts from FSS pages (e.g. https://www.uwcsea.edu.sg/fs/pages/4566) | school pages | n.d. | confidence: likely | class: regulatory
- [SG][attendance] No attendance reporting duty was found on the ICA FSS page. The page links to a separate "Student's Pass Cancellation" page, which was not read. | confidence: unverified | class: attendance
- [SG][vetting] Not researched (budget). Unverified belief: private and international schools must deploy teachers that meet CPE/EduTrust teacher-deployment criteria, and there is no SPF "Certificate of Clearance" requirement specific to teachers. Identifiers are NRIC (citizens/PRs) and FIN (foreigners). The DP law is the PDPA 2012. International schools mostly run Aug–Jun; MOE schools run Jan–Nov. | confidence: unverified | class: vetting / identifier / dp-law / calendar

### Malaysia
- [MY][regulatory] Immigration's Student Pass covers **non-citizens aged 3 and above**, from pre-school up to higher education. For school level, the school must be approved by the **Private Education Division, MOE** (Bahagian Pendidikan Swasta). **International Schools** also need **MOHA (Ministry of Home Affairs) authorisation** to enrol international students. | source: https://www.imi.gov.my/index.php/en/main-services/pass/long-term-social-visit-pass/student-pass/ | Jabatan Imigresen Malaysia | n.d. (footer ©2021) | accessed 2026-11-02 | confidence: verified | class: regulatory
- [MY][regulatory] School-level Student Pass: **IMM.14** form (new and renewal) plus a valid MOHA approval letter for international student intake. A legally appointed school representative, a parent or a legal guardian may apply, at the **Student Pass Unit, Visa, Pass and Permit Division** (HQ, State or Branch Immigration Office) for the school's location. **EMGS/STARS are referenced only for higher education, not schools.** A Social Visit Pass for guardians costs **RM90**; guardians may not work. | source: same | confidence: verified | class: regulatory
- [MY][attendance] The Immigration page states no attendance or absence reporting duty for schools. | confidence: unverified (absence of evidence) | class: attendance
- [MY][vetting] The **Permit Mengajar** (teaching permit) application form from **JPN Selangor** lists "**F – Sekolah Antarabangsa**" among institution categories, requires a health examination form, and gives a **maximum validity of 5 years**. | source: https://pulami.upsi.edu.my/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/JPN-SELANGOR-BORANG-PERMIT.pdf | JPN Selangor form (hosted by UPSI) | 2023-09 | confidence: likely (search extract of an official form) | class: vetting
- [MY][vetting] The **Child Act (Amendment) 2016** (gazetted July 2016) created a confidential **Child Register** of people convicted of offences against children, managed by **JKM (Social Welfare Department)**. A broader screening system for schools, childcare and school-bus operators was announced in Nov 2018. | source: https://www.freemalaysiatoday.com/category/nation/2016/07/26/child-act-amendment-2016-gazetted ; https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2017/11/02/child-registry-use-cant-be-forced-says-rohani/1500571 ; https://fmtv5.freemalaysiatoday.com/category/nation/2018/11/12/new-system-to-help-screen-employees-dealing-with-kids | FMT, Malay Mail | 2016–2018 | confidence: verified (several independent news sources agree; status after 2018 unknown) | class: vetting

## Contradictions / surprises
- **SG: EduTrust is a hard gate for Student's Pass at FSS.** ICA says FSS and privately-funded schools must be EduTrust-certified for the application to be considered. This contradicts the common belief that CPE/EduTrust covers only Private Education Institutions, not foreign system schools. A tenant attribute like "EduTrust status/expiry" matters for SG.
- **MY: school Student Passes do not go through EMGS.** They are filed directly with Immigration (IMM.14) and need a MOHA approval letter specific to international schools. EMGS/STARS is for higher education only.
- **MY: the Child Register check is voluntary, not mandatory**, as of the 2017–2018 evidence. The Minister said use "can't be forced".
- **AU: two attendance thresholds.** 80% is the at-risk/intervention trigger, and 70% is the level at or above which a school may choose not to report (8.15). States may set a threshold above 80%. Study periods can be semesters, not terms.
- **AU: WWC validity conflicts for WA and TAS** (3 vs 5 years) across secondary sources.
- **AU census: reference period of 20 school days ending on census day**, so an attendance-derived enrolment status is needed, not just the roll on census day.
