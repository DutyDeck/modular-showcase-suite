## Contradictions / surprises
- **UDISE+ reference date is 31 March, not 30 September.** The brief's assumption is outdated from 2022-23 onward. Seed data and data-return requirements should use 31 March (end of the financial year).
- **No LK international school appears in the MoE census, and none is registered with MoE.** The LK "statutory return" requirement for international school tenants is essentially nil. The real external data touchpoints are Cambridge/Edexcel entries and DoE private-candidate O/L and A/L registration.
- APAAR moved from "mandatory" for 2026 exams to "mandatory with refused/NOGEN escape values", and is due to become a hard requirement for 2026-27 registration and LOC. Sources disagree on the timing (2026 exams vs 2026-27 session). Overseas CBSE schools are exempt. **Product: the APAAR field needs an enumerated status (ID / REFUSED / NOGEN) and a stored parental-denial document.**
- Sri Lanka's PDPA commencement has slipped repeatedly (2025-03-18 removed; now 2027-01-01 for Parts I and III only). Data subject rights (Part II) are not yet in force.
- The LK government school year restarted on 5 Jan 2026 and the 2027 year starts 4 Jan 2027, so the post-COVID drift appears to be resolved.
- The 2021 Minister's count (about 393 international schools) and the 2015 MoE figure (391) are similar. Older press gave about 200. Counts are unreliable because there is no registry.
